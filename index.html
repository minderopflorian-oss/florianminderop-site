About

I come from finance and I'm fairly risk-averse by nature . That is exactly why I want to understand autonomous fleets before they arrive : the vehicles, the charging, the rules and the funding .

So I started the Tesla Cybercab Fleet Owners community on X, where future fleet owners learn and plan together, out in the open . I never give financial advice ; we share what we learn .

Independent, not affiliated with Tesla, Inc.
