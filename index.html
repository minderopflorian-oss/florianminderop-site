Why Cybercab

For years I told anyone who would listen that electric cars were coming . They came . I believe the self-driving car is the next step and the one that really makes the difference .

I'm fairly risk-averse by nature, so I want to understand autonomous fleets before they arrive : the vehicles, the charging, the rules and the funding . That is why I started the Tesla Cybercab Fleet Owners community on X, where future fleet owners learn and plan together, out in the open . I never give financial advice ; we share what we learn .
